RAGBot Technologies

RAGBot Technologies is a software company that develops artificial intelligence, machine learning, cloud computing, and data analytics solutions.

The company was established in 2018 and has its headquarters in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

WORKING HOURS

The standard working hours are Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees receive a one-hour lunch break between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM.

LEAVE POLICY

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per year.

Annual leave requests should normally be submitted at least five working days before the planned leave date.

WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees may work from home for up to two days per week with prior approval from their manager.

EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance, paid annual leave, sick leave, professional development programs, and performance-based incentives.

The company may reimburse approved certification and training expenses up to INR 30,000 per financial year.

PERFORMANCE EVALUATION

Employee performance is formally evaluated twice each year.

DATA SECURITY

Employees must protect company and customer information from unauthorized access.

Company passwords must not be shared with other employees or external individuals.

Employees must use multi-factor authentication for systems that support it.

RESIGNATION POLICY

Employees who resign are required to provide a 60-day notice unless otherwise specified in their employment contract.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

The customer support team operates from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Monday through Saturday.

Critical technical issues are targeted for an initial response within two hours.
